The Mandelbrot Set

Each pixel represents a complex number C. Starting at Z = 0, the renderer repeatedly calculates:

Zₙ₊₁ = Zₙ² + C

If the magnitude of Z exceeds 2, the point escapes. Its escape speed determines its color. Points that remain bounded are shown in black.

Controls

Move the mouse to position the zoom preview. Scroll to resize it. Click to zoom into that exact point. Hold the mouse wheel button and drag to pan. Press Escape to close this window.
